The Priestess

Song lyrics by Peter Mangiaracina

Written by Peter Mangiaracina

You portable nightmare, you cheapen the streets.
The souls of the gutter congeal at your feet.
Your arrangement of terror is shocking to hear.
Shuffling through shame has become your career.

I know where you get your vigor and verve,
But baby, you’ve got your nerve.

I’m so sick of being your fatted calf.
Your sacrificial grimaces — they make me laugh.
You are so wrapped up in your ambiance,
Your worthless self-image so firmly ensconced.

I know where you get your vigor and verve,
But baby, you’ve got your nerve.

Your language of loving confuses my head.
Your dent in my mattress despoils my bed.
With that sheet wrapped around you, you look so severe,
Like a hag in a madhouse, the priestess of tears.

I know where you get your vigor and verve,
But baby, you’ve got your nerve.

You are sweet, but it’s a put-on. You’ll conquer the herd,
Ignoring the gravesite where truth is interred.
You’re coughing up wisdom like phlegm from your throat.
You’ll poison their tonics and sit back and gloat.

I know where you get your vigor and verve,
But baby, you’ve got your nerve.

I’ve moved through your circles. I know how you work.
You fancy yourself a seer — do you think I’m a jerk?
Don’t you know that I thought you were lost in a fog
When you said you got mental impressions from dogs?

I know where you get your vigor and verve,
But baby, you’ve got your nerve.

The bright, bashful pilgrim — he falls at your feet.
His forehead is begging for something to eat.
In a month’s time, the hunger has fled from his eyes,
Persuaded professionally by your lies.

I know where you get your vigor and verve,
But baby, you’ve got your nerve.

You came into my life like a knife through wet flesh.
Our styles of believing became so enmeshed.
But I’m happy to say that I kept my heart new
While I watched as you scavenged through patterns of fools.

I know where you get your vigor and verve,
But baby, you’ve got your nerve.
