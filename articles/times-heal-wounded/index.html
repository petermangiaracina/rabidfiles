Time’s Heal Wounded

Song lyrics by Peter Mangiaracina

Written by Peter Mangiaracina

(The Wright Brothers – December 22, 1983)

Verse 1

I miss you, babe, I miss you like my skin.
You protected all that’s good in me against the maudlin.
And now I’m playing solitaire with my spirit’s bastard kin.
I can isolate my loneliness, but I don’t know how to clue you in.

Verse 2

I miss you, babe, like a disconnected phone,
Like the call of extinction when the buffalo roam.
And when I get outside myself, I can barely make it home.
It’s like a rattlesnake behind a stone.

Chorus

I can isolate my loneliness,

Verse 3

I miss you, babe, like a drug without release,
Like the wide-eyed grip of trembling in the hallways of the free.
Ever closer will I scrutinize until I cannot even see.
But I can’t translate it properly.

Chorus

I can isolate my loneliness,

I can isolate my loneliness,
But I can’t translate it properly.

Verse 4

I miss you, babe, like a trick without the treat,
Like a sucker by the circus tent, I’ve discovered I’ve been fleeced.
If I turn against your memory, it becomes its own defeat.
What you sow, so shall you reap.

Chorus

I can isolate my loneliness,
I can isolate my loneliness,
I can isolate my loneliness,
But I just can’t give and take.

Verse 5

I miss you, babe, like a planet’s crippled spin.
Now the earth spins around the sun, and even that seems like a sin.
A black hole is what I am, my sweet, and you are the victim near my rim,
And I fear that it might do me in.

Bridge

Time heals all wounds, but what if you’re dead inside?
What do you say to that, Miss Proper Noun?
Make no mistake — love is an artifact,
A beautiful mirage on a desert lake.

Final Chorus / Outro

I can isolate my loneliness,
I can isolate my loneliness,
I can isolate my loneliness,
But I just can’t give and take.
