Snail Fork

Song lyrics by Peter Mangiaracina

Written by Peter Mangiaracina

I am not a champion of changes
For me the smallest changes are big deal
I don’t need them when my world is in upheaval
But especially when I sit down to a meal

The waiter brought to me some small pink mollusks
They stared at me from deep within their shells
As I stared back with miserable revulsion
The waiter gave my food to someone else.

The place was packed with violent dinner noises
The din of plates the crash of silverware
I resigned myself to eating shellfish
With a knife and a fork bolt upright in my chair.
But what is this, this three pronged thing to eat with?
I would explain, but all description fails
Sandwiched in between my real utensils
Was a tiny little fork to pluck out snails

Snail fork, I don’t want to eat them
Snail fork, too hard to crunch on
Snail fork I don’t want to eat them
Snail fork too hard to crunch on

Well we all get used to things after a while
An irritating voice, a sense of doubt.
But I don’t think I ever will get used to
a little fork to pluck those suckers out

The people left,I sat alone at table
My eyes were closed, my soul was ripe with shame
I clenched my fists but then when I released them
The craziest snails went back to whence they came

Snail fork, too hard to crunch on
